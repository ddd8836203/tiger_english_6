## Part C: Write Right 3 (Unit 4) 提綱擴寫作文

> **大題說明**：
> `C. Michelle wrote the following outline. Help her to write her story in full down below.`
> `You will need to write complete sentences, and may need to add certain words to the outline:`
>
> 📋 **【提綱 Outline (Ground Truth)】**：
> - `Lived how long in neighbourhood?: four years`
> - `From school: quite close, under ten minutes on foot → Close to: convenience store, bus stop`
> - `Places: coffee shop → two blocks away / stationery store, pharmacy → across street from coffee shop`
> - `Favourite place: park → Activities: play with friends, ride my bike`

---

### 作文題目 (Title)
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：`________________________________________`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：`My Neighborhood`
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：打紅勾 `✓`
- **【標準正解】**：`My Neighborhood`

---

### 第 1 句：開篇提問 (Lead-in Question)
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：`Have you ever ________________ ?`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：`been to my neighborhood`
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：打紅勾 `✓`
- **【標準正解】**：`Have you ever been to my neighborhood?`
- **【教研 AI_SPEC 盲點診斷代碼】**：`OK_CORRECT`（現在完成式經驗提問句型 `Have you ever been to...` 掌握熟練）。

---

### 第 2 句：居住時間 (Length of Residency)
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：`I have lived ________________ .`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：`in my neighborhood for three years`
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：
  - 紅筆劃線刪除學生鉛筆手寫的 `three`，上方以紅筆改寫為 `four`
- **【標準正解】**：`I have lived in my neighborhood for four years.`
- **【教研 AI_SPEC 盲點診斷代碼】**：
  - `TRAP_OUTLINE_MISREAD_NUMBER`：審題疏漏，提綱明確給定 `four years`，學生因筆誤或記憶慣性寫成 `three years`。

---

### 第 3 句：與學校的距離與交通方式 (Distance & Commute)
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：`It is ________________ . I can get ________________ ten minutes on foot .`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：
  - 第一格：`very close to my school`（打紅勾 `✓`）
  - 第二格：`to school`
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：
  - 在 `to school` 後方插入紅筆符號，補寫紅字 `under`
- **【標準正解】**：`It is very close to my school. I can get to school in under ten minutes on foot.`
- **【教研 AI_SPEC 盲點診斷代碼】**：
  - `TRAP_PREPOSITION_UNDER_OMISSION`：遺漏提綱中的關鍵介系詞 `under`（表「在……時間以內」）。

---

### 第 4 句：周邊便利設施 (Nearby Amenities)
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：`The ________________ and the bus stop are very ________________ , too.`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：
  - 第一格：`convenience store`
  - 第二格：`closer`
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：
  - 在 `closer` 尾端的 `r` 上劃紅線刪除，改為原級 `close`；打紅勾 `✓`
- **【標準正解】**：`The convenience store and the bus stop are very close, too.`
- **【教研 AI_SPEC 盲點診斷代碼】**：
  - `TRAP_COMPARATIVE_DEGREE_OVERUSE`：平級同等描述語境中誤用比較級形式 `closer`（受上方 very 修飾時必須使用原級形容詞 `very close`）。

---

### 第 5 句：社區商店概況 (Stores Overview)
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：`There are ________________ .`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：`many stores in my neighborhood`
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：打紅勾 `✓`
- **【標準正解】**：`There are many stores in my neighborhood.`
- **【教研 AI_SPEC 盲點診斷代碼】**：`OK_CORRECT`（There are + 複數名詞 stores 搭配得當）。

---

### 第 6 句：具體店面位置描述 (Specific Store Locations)
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：`The ________________ across the street from the coffee shop ________________ , there is ________________ .`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：
  - 填答內容：
    `coffee shop are two blocks away from my house,`
    `across the street from the coffee shop, there is`
    `a stationery store and the pharmacy.`
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：打紅勾 `✓`
- **【標準正解】**：`The coffee shop is two blocks away from my house. Across the street from the coffee shop, there is a stationery store and a pharmacy.`
- **【教研 AI_SPEC 盲點診斷代碼】**：
  - `TRAP_SUBJECT_VERB_AGREEMENT`：學生寫 `coffee shop are`（主詞為單數 coffee shop，be 動詞應為 `is`）。

---

### 第 7 句：最喜愛的地點與戶外活動 (Favorite Place & Activities)
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：`Best of all, my ________________ . My friends and I often play ________________ .`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：
  - 第一格：`neighborhood has a park`
  - 第二格：`in the park. and ride`
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：
  - 第一格打紅勾 `✓`
  - 第二格：用紅筆劃掉學生手寫的 `in the park. and ride`，在上方插入紅字：`and ride our bikes`
- **【標準正解】**：`Best of all, my favorite place is the park. My friends and I often play and ride our bikes in the park.`
- **【教研 AI_SPEC 盲點診斷代碼】**：
  - `TRAP_SENTENCE_FRAGMENT_AND_RUN_ON`：學生將動詞片語斷句錯誤（寫成 `play in the park. and ride`），老師及時修正為平行對稱結構 `play and ride our bikes`。

---

### 第 8 句：總結結尾 (Concluding Sentence)
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：`I really ________________ .`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：`like living in my neighborhood`
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：打紅勾 `✓`
- **【標準正解】**：`I really like living in my neighborhood.`
- **【教研 AI_SPEC 盲點診斷代碼】**：`OK_CORRECT`（like + V-ing living 結構精準）。
