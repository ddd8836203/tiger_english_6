## Part A: Reading with Writing 6 (Unit 6) : DICTATION

> **教師指導語 (Instructions to Teacher)**：
> `INSTRUCTIONS TO TEACHER: Dictate the first two paragraphs on page 40 in their entirety.`
> `i. Do not mention punctuation of any kind whatsoever. Allow the students to judge what punctuation goes where based solely upon the natural intonation and pauses of your voice. Do not over-exaggerate the intonation; students need to refine their listening skills to natural native speaking.`
> `ii. Do not provide the spellings of words. The students need to hone their spelling abilities.`
> `iii. Do not over-repeat sentences. Students need to develop their ability to listen, retain the sentence in memory, and then write it out. The aim is to get them to do this at the sentence level, not the individual word level.`
>
> **答題指示**：
