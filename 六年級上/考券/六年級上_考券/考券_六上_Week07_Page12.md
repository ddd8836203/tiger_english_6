> `B. Write sentences using the given words. (Hint: Think about your verb tenses!):`
> **大題得分**：`8 / 10`

|:---:|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 1 | `1. [Roger, Sean – see new Iron Man movie tomorrow / so]` | `Roger see new Iron Man movie tomorrow, so do Sean.` | 在 `Roger` 與 `see` 間插入紅字 `will`；圈起 `do` 下方改寫 `will` | `Roger will see a new Iron Man movie tomorrow, and so will Sean.` | `TRAP_FUTURE_TOMORROW_MISSING_WILL`（忽略時間副詞 tomorrow 應使用未來式 will，導致附和助動詞也誤用 do） |
| 2 | `2. [me, my sister – go to bed early last night / too]` | `I went to bed early last night, my sister went to bed early, too.` | 打紅勾 `✓`（完全正確，亦可用 `my sister did, too`） | `I went to bed early last night, and my sister did, too.` | OK_CORRECT |
| 3 | `3. [Rachel, Nicole – not learn to play the piano / either]` | `Rachel do not learn to play the piano, Nicole do not play the piano, either.` | 兩處 `do not` 皆以紅筆框起，上方改寫 `doesn't` | `Rachel doesn't learn to play the piano, and Nicole doesn't, either.` | `TRAP_3RD_PERSON_SINGULAR_DOESNT`（第三人稱單數 Rachel / Nicole 否定助動詞必須用 doesn't 而非 do not） |
| 4 | `4. [penguins, ostriches – can't fly / neither]` | `Penguins can't fly, neither do ostriches.` | 紅筆框起 `do`，下方改寫 `can` | `Penguins can't fly, and neither can ostriches.` | `TRAP_MODAL_CAN_AUXILIARY_MISMATCH`（前句使用情態助動詞 can't，倒裝附和必須對應 neither can，不可慣性寫成 neither do） |

## Part C: Grammar 3 (Unit 14) — Comparative & Superlative Adverbs

> **大題說明**：
> `C. Answer the question to compare the three given items by writing a sentence with an adverb clause, a comparative adverb clause, and a superlative adverb clause. (The verb tense of your answer should match that of the question):`
> `Sentence form: <adverb clause>, + <comparative clause>, + but + <superlative clause>.`
> **大題得分**：`16 / 20`

| 題號 | 【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】 | 【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】 | 【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】 | 【標準正解】 | AI_SPEC 盲點診斷代碼 |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 1 | `1. How beautifully do they sing? Singing Scores: Anne: 10/10, Penny: 9/10 & Eric: 8/10` | `Penny sings more beautifully than Eric, but Anne is the most beautifully` | 紅筆劃除 `Anne` 後方的 `is`，下方改寫 `sings` | `(Eric sings beautifully,) Penny sings more beautifully than Eric, but Anne sings the most beautifully.` | `TRAP_SUPERLATIVE_VERB_REPLACED_BY_IS`（最高級子句結尾誤將一般動詞 sings 換成 be 動詞 is） |
| 2 | `2. How well did they do their homework? Jeremy (boy): 100%, Ingrid (girl): 95% & Hank (boy): 90%` | `Ingrid did will than Hana, but Jeremy do the best.` | 圈起 `will` 下方改寫 `better`；圈起 `Hana` 改 `Hank`；劃除 `do` 下方改寫 `did` | `(Hank did his homework well,) Ingrid did better than Hank, but Jeremy did the best.` | `TRAP_IRREGULAR_WELL_TO_BETTER_AND_PAST_DID`（well 比較級應為 better 誤寫成 will；過去式問句後半句忘記維持過去式 did） |
| 3 | `3. How easily do you learn: Chinese, science & maths?` | `Science is easilier than maths, but Chinese is the easiliest.` | 句首補紅字 `I can learn`；劃掉 `is easilier` 改 `more easily`；後半句改寫為 `I can learn Chinese the most easily.` | `I learn maths easily, I learn science more easily than maths, but I learn Chinese the most easily.` | `TRAP_ADVERB_EASILY_TREATED_AS_ADJECTIVE`（將副詞 easily 誤當形容詞自創 easilier/easiliest，且漏寫主詞與一般動詞 I learn） |
| 4 | `4. How badly do they play the electric guitar? Guitar Scores: Bill: 1/10, Ted: 3/10 & Fred: 5/10` | `Ted plays the electric guitar badly than Fred, but Bill play the most badly` | 在 `badly` 前插入 `more` 或改寫 `worse`；`Bill play` 補 `s`；`most badly` 劃掉改 `worst` | `(Fred plays the electric guitar badly,) Ted plays worse than Fred, but Bill plays the worst.` | `TRAP_IRREGULAR_BADLY_WORSE_WORST`（badly 為不規則副詞，比較級與最高級為 worse / the worst，絕對不可寫成 badly than 或 most badly） |
| 5 | `5. How early did they get up? Elizabeth: 5:30 a.m., Caroline: 6:00 a.m. & Melanie: 6:30 a.m.` | `Caroline gets up earlier than Melanie, but Elizebeth is the earliest.` | 紅筆劃除 `is`，下方改寫 `gets up`（註：題幹為過去式 did they get up，標準亦可用 got up） | `(Melanie got up early,) Caroline got up earlier than Melanie, but Elizabeth got up the earliest.` | `TRAP_SUPERLATIVE_VERB_REPLACED_BY_IS`（再次於最高級子句把一般動詞 get up 誤換成 be 動詞 is） |
