## Part C: Write Right 3 (Unit 5) — 14 Errors Proofreading

> **大題說明**：
> `C. Correct the fourteen errors in the text below. Indicate each mistake, and add the corrections to the text. (Hint: Look for errors in capitalisation, punctuation, spelling, plurals, pronouns, missing words, incorrect words, verb tense, articles (a/an/the) and subject-verb agreement):`
> **大題得分**：`5 / 7`

### 1. 【Layer 1: 原始印刷待改錯短文 (含 14 處預設錯誤)】
```text
If I Have 3000 Dollars
If I had 3000 dollar, I would do three thing.
First, my family loves baseball, so I would go a baseball game with him. Next, I buy a fish tank for my class. I think it would brighten up us's classroom. First, I would spend some money on meself. I would by some comic book. They would spend me about $300.
With rest of money, I would treat my friends to lunch.
```

### 2. 【Layer 2 (學生鉛筆訂正) vs. Layer 3 (老師紅筆批改) 14 處改錯逐點對照表】

| 序號 | 原文錯誤片段 | 【Layer 2: 學生鉛筆作答】 | 【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改】 | 【標準正解】 | 學生是否抓對 | 盲點解析 |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| 1 | 標題 `If I Have 3000 Dollars` | 劃掉 `Have` 改寫 `Had` | 打紅勾 `✓` | `Had` | ✅ 抓對 | 與現在事實相反假設語氣標題用 `If I Had...` |
| 2 | `3000 dollar` | 劃掉 `dollar` 改寫 `dollars` | 打紅勾 `✓` | `dollars` | ✅ 抓對 | 3000 後方複數名詞加 `-s` |
| 3 | `three thing` | 劃掉 `thing` 改寫 `things` | 打紅勾 `✓` | `things` | ✅ 抓對 | three 後方複數名詞加 `-s` |
| 4 | `go a baseball game` | ❌ **未發現漏字** | 紅筆於 `go` 後畫插入號補寫 `to` | `go to a baseball game` | ❌ **漏抓** | `go` 為不及物動詞，接地點/活動名詞必須加介系詞 `to` |
| 5 | `with him` (指 family) | 劃掉 `him` 改寫 `them` | 打紅勾 `✓` | `them` | ✅ 抓對 | 指代家人們 (`my family` 成員) 受格用 `them` |
| 6 | `Next, I buy` | 在 `I` 與 `buy` 間插入 `would` | 打紅勾 `✓` | `Next, I would buy` | ✅ 抓對 | 假設語氣全文主要子句皆須維持 `would + V` |
| 7 | `I think it would` | ❌ 誤將正確的 `it` 改成 `you` | 紅筆劃掉學生多改的 `you` | `it` (原字正確) | ❌ **誤改** | `it` 指代前句的 `a fish tank`，本來就是對的 |
| 8 | `us's classroom` | 先寫 `our's` 後改寫 `our` | 紅筆圈掉 `our's`，對 `our` 打勾 | `our classroom` | ✅ 抓對 | 形容詞性所有格為 `our`，絕對沒有 `us's` 或 `our's` |
| 9 | 第二個 `First,` | 劃掉 `First` 改寫 `Finally` | 打紅勾 `✓` | `Finally,` | ✅ 抓對 | 順序轉折詞：First ➔ Next ➔ Finally |
| 10 | `on meself` | 劃掉 `meself` 改寫 `myself` | 打紅勾 `✓` | `myself` | ✅ 抓對 | 第一人稱反身代名詞為 `myself` |
| 11 | `I would by` | ❌ **未發現同音異字錯誤** | 紅筆圈起 `by` 改寫 `buy` | `I would buy` | ❌ **漏抓** | 介系詞 `by` 與動詞 `buy` (購買) 同音異字混淆 |
| 12 | `some comic book` | 劃掉 `book` 改寫 `books` | 打紅勾 `✓` | `books` | ✅ 抓對 | `some` 後接可數名詞複數 `comic books` |
| 13 | `They would spend me` | 劃掉 `spend` 改寫 `cost`（另誤將 `They` 改 `It's` 與句尾加 `dollars`） | 對 `cost` 打勾，劃掉多改的 `It's` 與重複的 `dollars` | `They would cost me` | ✅ 抓對核心 | 物品當主詞時花費動詞用 `cost` 不用 `spend`；已有 `$` 符號不必再寫 `dollars` |
| 14 | `With rest of money` | 僅在 `of` 後補 `the` (`rest of the money`) | 紅筆在 `With` 與 `rest` 間再補插入 `the` | `With the rest of the money` | ⚠️ **半對** | 固定片語『用剩下的錢』必須有兩個定冠詞：`With the rest of the money` |
