## Part B: Grammar 3 (Unit 14) : 副詞三級比較長句合成

> **大題說明**：
> `B. Answer the question to compare the three given items by writing a sentence with an adverb clause, a comparative adverb clause, and a superlative adverb clause. (The verb tense of your answer should match that of the question):`
>
> 📐 **【句型公式 Sentence form】**：
> `<adverb clause> , + <comparative clause> , + but + <superlative clause> .`
>
> **大題得分**：`10 / 12`（3 題每題 4 分，學生失分 2 分）

---

### 第 1 題
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：
  `1. How fast do they run? person, dog & lion`
  `________________________________________________________________________________`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：
  `A dog runs faster than a person, but lion can run fastest.`
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：
  - 在 `lion` 前方以紅筆插入符號補寫 `a`（打紅勾 `✓`）。
  - 在 `fastest` 前方以紅筆插入符號補寫 `the`。
- **【標準正解】**：
  `A person runs fast, a dog runs faster than a person, but a lion runs the fastest.`
- **【教研 AI_SPEC 盲點診斷代碼】**：
  - `TRAP_ARTICLE_OMISSION_A`：單數可數名詞 lion 漏加不定冠詞 a。
  - `TRAP_SUPERLATIVE_THE_OMISSION`：最高級副詞 fastest 前漏加定冠詞 the（學生雖在頂部草稿寫了 the est，答題時仍漏掉）。
  - `TRAP_MODAL_VERB_INSERTION`：題幹問 `do they run`，學生後半句突然多出情態助動詞 `can run`。
- **【得分判定】**：3 / 4 分。

---

### 第 2 題
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：
  `2. How quickly can they walk? five year old, eight year old & adult`
  `________________________________________________________________________________`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：
  `An eight year old can walk more than a five year old, but an adult can walk the most quickly.`
  *(注意：學生中間比較級漏寫了副詞 quickly，寫成 walk more than)*
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：
  - 在 `more` 後方以紅筆插入符號 `^ quickly`，並框選學生手寫的 `walk`。
- **【標準正解】**：
  `A five year old can walk quickly, an eight year old can walk more quickly than a five year old, but an adult can walk the most quickly.`
- **【教研 AI_SPEC 盲點診斷代碼】**：
  - `TRAP_ADVERB_OMISSION_IN_COMPARATIVE`：多音節副詞比較級漏寫副詞本體，誤寫為 `more than`（正確應為 `more quickly than`）。
  - `SUCCESS_SUPERLATIVE_STRUCTURE`：後半句 `the most quickly` 結構完整正確。
- **【得分判定】**：3 / 4 分。

---

### 第 3 題
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：
  `3. How far can they jump? frog, rabbit & kangaroo`
  `________________________________________________________________________________`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：
  `A rabbit can jump further than a frog, but kangaroo can jump the furthest.`
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：
  - 在 `kangaroo` 前方以紅筆插入符號補寫 `a`（打紅勾 `✓`）。
  - 老師圈選了兩處動詞 `jump`，確認助動詞 can 後方為原形動詞。
- **【標準正解】**：
  `A frog can jump far, a rabbit can jump farther than a frog, but a kangaroo can jump the farthest.`
- **【教研 AI_SPEC 盲點診斷代碼】**：
  - `TRAP_ARTICLE_OMISSION_A`：袋鼠 kangaroo 前漏加不定冠詞 a。
  - `SUCCESS_IRREGULAR_FAR`：成功寫出 `further / farthest`（或 farther / farthest）不規則變化。
- **【得分判定】**：4 / 4 分（扣微瑕疵綜合得分 10/12）。

## Part C: Write Right 3 (Unit 5) 假設語氣與預算分配作文

> **大題說明**：
> `C. Nathaniel wrote the following outline. Help him to write his story in full down below.`
> `You will need to write complete sentences, and may need to add certain words to the outline:`
>
> 📋 **【提綱 Outline (Ground Truth)】**：
> - `Amount: 6000 dollars`
> - `Do 1: amusement park with friends → (Why?) love fair rides`
> - `Do 2: share some with sister → (Why?) important to share → family`
> - `Do 3: spend on me → new jacket (about $750) → treat friends to movie`
>
> **大題得分**：`15 / 16`（扣 1 分）

---

### 作文題目 (Title)
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：`________________________________________`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：`If I Had 6000 Dollars`
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：認可通過
- **【標準正解】**：`If I Had 6000 Dollars`

---

### 第 1 句：假設條件與總綱 (Topic Sentence)
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：`If I had ________________ , I would ________________ things:`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：
  - 第一格：`6000 dollars`
  - 第二格：`do three`（紅筆打勾 `✓`）
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：在 `do three` 後打紅勾
- **【標準正解】**：`If I had 6000 dollars, I would do three things:`
- **【教研 AI_SPEC 盲點診斷代碼】**：`OK_CORRECT`（與現在事實相反之假設語氣 If I had..., I would do... 掌握精準）。

---

### 第 2 句：第一項花費（遊樂園與好友）(First Item: Amusement Park)
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：`First, ________________ , so I ________________ amusement park with my friends.`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：
  - 第一格：`I would play fair rides`
  - 第二格：`would go to`
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：
  - 在 `amusement` 前方以紅筆插入符號，補寫紅字 `an`
- **【標準正解】**：`First, I love fair rides, so I would go to an amusement park with my friends.`
- **【教研 AI_SPEC 盲點診斷代碼】**：
  - `TRAP_ARTICLE_AN_OMISSION`：amusement 為母音 /ə/ 開頭，漏加不定冠詞 an（寫成 go to amusement park）。

---

### 第 3 句：第二項花費（與妹妹分享）(Second Item: Share with Sister)
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：`Next, ________________ .`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：`I would share some with sister.`
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：
  - 在 `sister` 前方以紅筆插入符號，補寫紅字 `my`
- **【標準正解】**：`Next, I would share some of the money with my sister.`
- **【教研 AI_SPEC 盲點診斷代碼】**：
  - `TRAP_POSSESSIVE_PRONOUN_OMISSION`：稱呼家庭成員漏加所有格代名詞 my（寫成 with sister 而非 with my sister）。

---

### 第 4 句：分享理念與原因說明 (Rationale: Share with Family)
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：`I think ________________ .`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：
  `share money with my family.`
  *(備註：學生鉛筆原答句子缺乏主詞與動詞結構，僅寫出動詞原形片語)*
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：
  - 老師用紅筆重重劃掉學生手寫內容，上方完整改寫為紅字：
    `it's important to share money with your family`
- **【標準正解】**：`I think it is important to share money with your family.`
- **【教研 AI_SPEC 盲點診斷代碼】**：
  - `TRAP_CLAUSE_STRUCTURE_IT_IS_IMPORTANT`：提綱中給定 `important to share → family`，學生未轉換為虛主詞名詞子句 `it is important to + V`，直接寫 `share money...`，喪失句子結構完整性。

---

### 第 5 句：第三項花費（個人夾克與電影）(Third Item: Jacket & Movie)
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：`Finally, ________________ . A new jacket would ________________ me ________________ .`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：
  - 第一格：`I would spend 750 dollars on myself.`
  - 第二格：`cost`
  - 第三格：`750 dollars.`（紅筆打勾 `✓`）
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：在 `cost me 750 dollars` 處打紅勾 `✓`
- **【標準正解】**：`Finally, I would spend some money on myself. A new jacket would cost me about 750 dollars.`
- **【教研 AI_SPEC 盲點診斷代碼】**：`OK_CORRECT`（spend money on oneself 與 S + cost + sb + money 句型掌握熟練）。

---

### 第 6 句：剩餘款項請客 (Rest of the Money)
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：`With the rest of ________________ .`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：`the money, I would treat my friends to movie.`
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：認可通過
- **【標準正解】**：`With the rest of the money, I would treat my friends to a movie.`
- **【教研 AI_SPEC 盲點診斷代碼】**：`OK_CORRECT`（treat someone to a movie 搭配詞精準）。

---

### 第 7 句：反問總結 (Rhetorical Closing)
- **【Layer 1: 原始空白題幹】**：`Wouldn't it ________________ ?`
- **【Layer 2: 學生原始作答（純鉛筆）】**：`be great to have 6000 dollars.`
- **【Layer 3: 老師紅筆批改（紅筆）】**：打大紅勾 `✓`
- **【標準正解】**：`Wouldn't it be great to have 6000 dollars?`
- **【教研 AI_SPEC 盲點診斷代碼】**：`OK_EXCELLENT`（否定反問句 Wouldn't it be great to... 掌握完美）。
