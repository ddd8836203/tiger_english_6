### 1. Introduction & Ancient Astronomers (Student Book p.20)

**Astronomy** is studying the planets and the stars, and people who study astronomy are called **astronomers**. Do you know what they have discovered?

#### Ancient Astronomers
Thousands of years ago, people in places like China, Iraq, and Greece used the Sun, the Moon, and the stars to calculate the time of year and the seasons. This is how they knew the dates of festivals and when to plant seeds. Many people, like the Ancient Egyptians or the Incas of South America, thought that the Sun was a god. They built beautiful temples for the Sun.

> **[Photo Caption p.20]**: `Inside Abu Simbel Temple` *(圖解：埃及阿布辛貝神廟內部石雕神像，陽光每年僅在兩天破曉時分直射神廟深處)*

> [!NOTE] **Discover! Box (p.20)**
> Sunlight falls on the statues in this Ancient Egyptian temple at sunrise on two days every year. These were important days in the Egyptian calendar.

### 2. Understanding the Solar System (Student Book p.21)

Most ancient astronomers believed that Earth was at the center of our solar system. They thought that the Sun, the other stars, and all the planets orbited Earth.

> **[Photo Caption p.21]**: `An Ancient Map of the Planets` *(圖解：17 世紀托勒密地心說古代宇宙地圖，描繪地球居中、日月行星環繞運行的同心圓軌道)*

In 1543, a Polish astronomer called **Copernicus** said that the Sun was at the center of our solar system, but people didn't believe him. Later, other astronomers like **Galileo Galilei**, from Pisa, now in Italy, believed him, but important people stopped them writing about their ideas. Almost 150 years later, a British astronomer called **Isaac Newton** proved that Earth orbits the Sun.

> [!NOTE] **Discover! Box (p.21)**
> Isaac Newton discovered the force of gravity. Gravity makes things fall to the ground on Earth. It also makes the planets orbit the Sun. *(插圖：牛頓坐在蘋果樹下，被落下的蘋果啟發發現重力)*

### 3. Telescopes, Galaxies and the Universe (Student Book p.22)

#### Galileo and the First Telescopes
Telescopes help people to learn about our solar system. In 1609, Galileo used one of the first telescopes to look at the night sky. He saw mountains and craters on the Moon, and four moons around Jupiter. The first telescopes were very small. Later, people made bigger telescopes, and they discovered new planets and moons.

> **[Illustration Caption p.22]**: *(圖解：伽利略在文藝復興時期的書房窗邊，手持木製單筒望遠鏡凝視夜空星體並繪製星圖手稿)*

#### Our Galaxy and the Universe
When Galileo looked at a bright area in the night sky, he discovered that it was made of many stars. Later, people discovered that this is the center of our galaxy, the **Milky Way**. For a long time, astronomers thought that the Milky Way was the only galaxy in the universe. Then in 1930, an American astronomer called **Edwin Hubble** discovered another galaxy. Now we know that there are billions of galaxies in the universe.

> **[Photo Caption p.22]**: `The Center of the Milky Way` *(圖解：銀河系核心璀璨壯闊的密集成千上萬億恆星光帶與深邃星雲)*

### 4. Astronomy Today (Student Book p.23)

Today, astronomers use huge telescopes to study the stars. They can see the light from galaxies billions of kilometers from Earth. Astronomers use different telescopes to look for other types of energy from stars, called **radio waves** and **X-rays**. Astronomers also use **spacecraft** to explore our solar system. Spacecraft orbit or fly past other planets and take photos. Some spacecraft have visited other planets and moons, too.

> [!NOTE] **Discover! Box (p.23)**
> The Hubble Space Telescope has orbited Earth since 1990. It can take better photos than telescopes on Earth because it's outside Earth's atmosphere.

> **[Photo Caption p.23]**: 
> 1. `A Hubble Space Photo` *(圖解：哈伯太空望遠鏡拍攝之鷹星雲「創世之柱」深空壯麗星雲，彩色氣體塵埃柱)*
> 2. `Hubble Space Telescope` *(圖解：在太空中展開太陽能板繞地運行的圓筒狀哈伯太空望遠鏡本體)*

#### Activity 3: Complete the sentences. (p.45)
*Word Bank*: `discovered` | `believed` | `Sun` | `universe` | `gravity` | `believed` | `important` | `proved`

1. Copernicus said that everything in the solar system orbited the **Sun**, but nobody **believed** him.
2. Galileo **believed** Copernicus, but **important** people didn't want him to write about his ideas.
3. Newton discovered the force of **gravity**. He **proved** that Copernicus was right.
4. Hubble **discovered** another galaxy. Now we know that there are lots of galaxies in the **universe**.

#### Activity 4: Write true or false. (p.45)
1. Ancient people used the stars to calculate things. ➔ **true** *(用日月星辰計算季節與農耕曆法)*
2. Galileo thought that the Earth orbited the Sun. ➔ **true** *(伽利略支持哥白尼日心說)*
3. Gravity makes planets orbit the Sun. ➔ **true** *(牛頓萬有引力支配天體軌道)*
4. The Milky Way is made of billions of comets. ➔ **false** *(銀河系是由無數恆星組成，非彗星)*
5. Hubble discovered another universe. ➔ **false** *(哈伯發現的是另一個星系 galaxy，非 universe)*
