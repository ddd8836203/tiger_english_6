### 1. Chapter 4: Rocks and Ice (Student Book p.16)

There are two rings around the Sun, called the **asteroid belt** and the **Kuiper belt**. The asteroid belt is between Mars and Jupiter. It's made of rocks called **asteroids**. The Kuiper belt is further away than Neptune. It's made of pieces of ice.

#### Asteroids
Some rocks in the asteroid belt are the size of houses, but others are many kilometers across. When planets come near them, or other asteroids hit them, these rocks can leave the asteroid belt. Sometimes, they travel toward the inner planets.

> **[Photo Caption p.16]**: `The Asteroid Belt` *(圖解：黑暗太空中漂浮著無數大小不一的深褐色岩石碎片，圍繞太陽公轉)*

### 2. Comets (Student Book p.17)

**Comets** are huge pieces of ice and dust, and some of them are 50 kilometers across. Comets come from the Kuiper belt, or from further away in space, but sometimes their orbits bring them nearer Earth and the Sun. When comets come nearer the Sun, they become hotter and they leave pieces of ice and dust behind them. This is called a **comet's tail** and it can be millions of kilometers long!

> **[Photo Caption p.17]**: `Halley's Comet in 1986` *(圖解：1986 年拍攝的哈雷彗星，核心發出耀眼藍白色強光，後方拖曳出數百萬公里長、散佈微塵的壯麗彗尾)*

> [!NOTE] **Discover! Box (p.17)**
> Halley's Comet is a famous comet. It comes near Earth every 75 or 76 years. We will see it again in 2061.

---

### 3. Meteors and Meteorites (Student Book p.18)

**Meteors** are pieces of rock, dust, or ice that enter a planet's atmosphere. They come from asteroids and comets.

Most meteors burn when they enter the atmosphere, but sometimes pieces hit a planet's surface and these pieces are called **meteorites**. More than 1,000 metric tons of ice, dust, and rocks from space land on Earth every year, but most of these pieces are very small.

Sometimes big meteorites hit Earth and make a huge hole called a **crater**. Big meteorites don't hit planets very often, but they are very dangerous. In 1994, 21 pieces of a comet hit Jupiter. Some of the explosions were bigger than our planet Earth!

> **[Photo Caption p.18]**: `A Meteorite Crater in Arizona, USA` *(圖解：美國亞利桑那州巴林傑隕石坑，直徑超過 1.2 公里的巨大碗狀凹坑，展現天體撞擊的巨大威力)*

### 4. Historic Impacts & Dinosaur Extinction (Student Book p.19)

> [!NOTE] **Discover! Box (p.19)**
> In 1908, a meteorite exploded in the sky above the Tunguska River, in Siberia in Russia. No one lived there, but a forest of 80 million trees was burned.

About 65 million years ago, there were no people on Earth, and the animals were different, too. Dinosaurs lived here. Then one day a bright light appeared in the sky. It was a huge meteorite. Some of it burned in the atmosphere, but a piece hit Earth – it was about 20 kilometers long. The meteorite crater is in Chicxulub, in the Yucatan in Mexico, and it's 180 kilometers across!

When the meteorite hit Earth, there was a huge explosion. Scientists think that fires and acid destroyed most of the world's forests. Clouds of dust covered the sky, and light from the Sun didn't reach Earth's surface. It became very cold for many years. At about the same time, the dinosaurs and 70% of all the plants and other animals on Earth died.

> **[Photo Caption p.19]**: `The Chicxulub Meteorite` *(圖解：約 6500 萬年前，一條巨大且熾熱燃燒的巨大隕石劃過天空墜向藍色海洋，前景為一隻展翅翱翔但即將面臨滅絕命運的翼手龍)*

### 5. Chapter 4 Activities (Student Book p.42 - p.43)

#### Activity 1: Write the words. (p.42)
*Word Bank*: `meteor` | `comet` | `meteorite` | `asteroid`

1. This is made of ice and dust. ➔ **comet**
2. This is a big rock. ➔ **asteroid**
3. This is a piece of rock, dust, or ice that enters the atmosphere. ➔ **meteor**
4 This is a piece of ice or rock that hits a planet. ➔ **meteorite**

#### Activity 2: Complete the sentences. (p.42)
*Word Bank*: `ice` | `crater` | `dinosaurs` | `tail` | `rings` | `rocks` | `burn`

1. There are two **rings** around the Sun.
2. The asteroid belt is made of **rocks**.
3. The Kuiper belt is made of pieces of **ice**.
4. When a comet comes near the Sun, it has a **tail**.
5. Most meteors **burn** when they enter the atmosphere.
6. A big meteorite can make a **crater** on Earth.
7. The **dinosaurs** were big animals that lived a long time ago.

#### Activity 3: Write T for the Tunguska meteorite or C for the Chicxulub meteorite. (p.43)
This meteorite ...
1. hit Earth millions of years ago. ➔ **C** *(約 6500 萬年前)*
2. destroyed a forest. ➔ **T** *(燒毀西伯利亞 8000 萬棵樹木)*
3. was about 20 kilometers long. ➔ **C** *(撞擊碎片約 20 公里長)*
4. killed a lot of animals. ➔ **C** *(滅絕恐龍與 70% 動植物)*
5. exploded in the sky. ➔ **T** *(在通古斯河上空爆炸)*
6. changed the weather for a long time. ➔ **C** *(塵埃蔽日，多年嚴寒)*
7. hit Russia about 100 years ago. ➔ **T** *(1908 年發生於俄羅斯西伯利亞)*

#### Activity 4: Correct the sentences. (p.43)
1. The asteroid belt is between Earth and the Sun.
   - **Correction**: `The asteroid belt is between Mars and Jupiter.`
2. A lot of comets come from the asteroid belt.
   - **Correction**: `A lot of comets come from the Kuiper belt (or from further away in space).`
3. Comets become colder when they are near the Sun.
   - **Correction**: `Comets become hotter when they are near the Sun.`
4. We can see Halley's Comet in the sky all the time.
   - **Correction**: `We can see Halley's Comet every 75 or 76 years (we will see it again in 2061).`
5. Some meteorites hit the Moon in 1994.
   - **Correction**: `21 pieces of a comet hit Jupiter in 1994.`
6. About 65 million years ago, there were no animals on Earth.
   - **Correction**: `About 65 million years ago, there were no people on Earth (dinosaurs lived here).`
