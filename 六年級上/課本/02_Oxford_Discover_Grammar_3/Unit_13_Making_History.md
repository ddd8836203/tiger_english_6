### 【p.68 學習文法 Learn Grammar】

#### 核心文法規則框：Learn Grammar — So, Too, Either, Neither
*(漫畫角色：右側有藍色火焰小精靈 Harry 露出生動驚喜的表情。)*

> **Remember!**
> - **Use *so* or *too* in a sentence to agree with something affirmative in another sentence.**
>   - Antonio Meucci and Johann Philipp Reis made telephone machines. **So did** Elisha Gray.
>   - Bell was trying to invent a telephone. Other scientists were trying to invent telephones, **too**.
> - **You can also use *too* in shorter sentences, like this:**
>   - Bell was trying to invent a telephone. Other scientists **were, too**.
> - **Use *either* or *neither* in a sentence to agree with something negative in another sentence.**
>   - Gray did not become rich and famous. Meucci didn't become rich and famous, **either**.
>   - Meucci didn't become rich and famous. **Neither did** Reis.
>
> 💡 **Grammar Tip!**
> - Use **so** and **neither** at the **beginning** of a sentence. *(句首倒裝)*
> - Use **too** and **either** at the **end** of a sentence, after a comma. *(句尾逗號後)*

#### B. Choose the correct words to complete the sentences.
1. Antonio Meucci was an inventor. Johann Philipp Reis was an inventor, _______.
   - a) so
   - b) either
   - c) **too** *(正解：c)*
2. Alexander Graham Bell didn't invent the television. He didn't invent the computer, _______.
   - a) neither
   - b) **either** *(正解：b)*
   - c) so
3. Elisha Gray did lots of experiments with telephone machines. _______ did Alexander Graham Bell.
   - a) **So** *(正解：a)*
   - b) Either
   - c) Neither
4. Johann Philipp Reis lived in the 19th century. Antonio Meucci lived in the 19th century, _______.
   - a) so
   - b) neither
   - c) **too** *(正解：c)*
5. Antonio Meucci wasn't from the U.K. _______ was Johann Philipp Reis.
   - a) Too
   - b) **Neither** *(正解：b)*
   - c) So
6. Elisha Gray invented other machines. _______ did Antonio Meucci.
   - a) **So** *(正解：a)*
   - b) Either
   - c) Too

- d. So was Edison's light bulb.

- f. Other scientists were experimenting with light bulbs, too.
- g. Joseph Swan was trying to invent a light bulb, too.

**篇章正文填空：**
> In 1878, Thomas Edison was trying to invent a light bulb. **¹ [g. Joseph Swan was trying to invent a light bulb, too.]**
> Edison and Swan weren't the only scientists to experiment with light bulbs. **² [f. Other scientists were experimenting with light bulbs, too.]** British inventor Humphry Davy invented a light bulb. **³ [c. So did Canadian inventor Henry Woodward.]** But their light bulbs were not very successful.
> Swan's light bulb was a success. **⁴ [d. So was Edison's light bulb.]** Swan's light bulb worked for a long time. **⁵ [a. So did Edison's light bulb.]**

> Swan wasn't happy about this. **⁷ [b. Edison wasn't happy, either.]** Swan and Edison weren't friends, but their inventions changed the world!

#### H. Let's Talk!
*(口語生活對話框：練習在日常表達相同觀點)*

> **實用語塊庫：**
> `Me too.` / `So am I.` / `Neither am I.` / `I'm not, either.`

**情境示範對話：**
- A: "I'm not eight years old."
- B: "Neither am I. I'm nine years old."
- A: "I like ice cream."
- B: "Me too! / So do I."

  concept_id: "ODG3_U13_AGREEMENT_SO_TOO_EITHER_NEITHER"
  target_grade: "Grade 6 Term 1 (小六上學期期中考關鍵考點)"
  cefr_level: "A2+ (銜接 B1)"
  
  linguistic_kernel:
    grammar_formulas:
      affirmative_agreement:
        rule_1_front: "So + 助動詞/be動詞 + 主詞. (例：So did Gray. / So was his father.)"
        rule_2_end: "主詞 + 助動詞/be動詞, too. (例：Gray did, too. / His father was, too.)"
      negative_agreement:
        rule_1_front: "Neither + 肯定助動詞/be動詞 + 主詞. (例：Neither did Reis. / Neither was he.)"
        rule_2_end: "主詞 + 否定助動詞/be動詞, either. (例：Reis didn't, either. / He wasn't, either.)"
    key_auxiliary_rules:
      be_verb: "前句有 be 動詞 (am/is/are/was/were)，附和句必用對應 be 動詞。"
      action_verb_past: "前句為一般動詞過去式 (invented, moved, lived)，附和句必用 did / didn't。"
      action_verb_present: "前句為一般動詞現在式，附和句用 do/does。"
      
  misconceptions_and_error_traps:
    - trap_id: "ERR_SO_TOO_POSITION"
      error_pattern: "*Too did Gray. / *Gray did, so."
      correction: "So 必放句首倒裝；too 必放句尾逗號後。"
      explanation: "小學生常將中文『也是』直譯，誤將 too 置於句首。"
      
    - trap_id: "ERR_NEITHER_DOUBLE_NEGATIVE"
      error_pattern: "*Neither didn't Reis. / *Neither was not he."
      correction: "Neither did Reis. / Neither was he."
      explanation: "Neither 本身已包含否定含義 (not either)，後方助動詞必須使用肯定形式，嚴禁雙重否定。"
      
    - trap_id: "ERR_EITHER_WITHOUT_NOT"
      error_pattern: "*Reis did, either."
      correction: "Reis didn't, either."
      explanation: "Either 放句尾時，前面的動詞必須帶有 not (didn't / wasn't / weren't)。"
      
    - trap_id: "ERR_AUXILIARY_VERB_MISMATCH"
      error_pattern: "Bell was a scientist. -> *So did Meucci. / Bell invented the telephone. -> *So was Gray."
      correction: "前句 was ➔ 後句 was；前句 invented ➔ 後句 did。"
      explanation: "最典型的台灣段考魔王陷阱：學生容易忽略前句是 be 動詞還是一般動詞，導致助動詞用錯。"
