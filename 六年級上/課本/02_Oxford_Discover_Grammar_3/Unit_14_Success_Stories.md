### 【p.72 學習文法 Learn Grammar】

#### 核心文法規則框：Learn Grammar — Adverbs, Comparative and Superlative Adverbs
*(漫畫角色：右側有紫色可愛小精靈 Mag 做出驚喜的指引手勢。)*

> **Remember!**
> - **Use adverbs to describe how a person or thing does something.**
>   - Jane sings **beautifully**.
> - **Here are some examples of how you form adverbs from adjectives:**
>   - quick ➔ **quickly**
>   - wonderful ➔ **wonderfully**
>   - horrible ➔ **horribly** *(去 e 加 y)*
>   - easy ➔ **easily** *(去 y 變 i 加 ly)*
> - **Some adverbs are irregular, for example:**
>   - good ➔ **well**
>   - far ➔ **far**
>   - fast ➔ **fast**
>   - early ➔ **early**
>   - hard ➔ **hard**
>   - high ➔ **high**
>   - late ➔ **late**
> - **When you compare the same action, use comparative or superlative adverbs.**
>   - The glider didn't fly very **far**.
>   - The first plane flew **further** than the glider.
>   - The second plane flew **the furthest**.
> - **Some comparative adverbs include *more* and *the most*:**
>   - The glider didn't fly very **quickly**.
>   - The first plane flew **more quickly** than the glider.
>   - The second plane flew **the most quickly**.
> - **Some comparative adverbs are irregular. Here are some common examples:**
>
> | Adverb | Comparative Adverb | Superlative Adverb |
> | :--- | :--- | :--- |
> | **well** | **better** | **the best** |
> | **badly** | **worse** | **the worst** |
> | **far** | **further** | **the furthest** |

#### B. Complete the text. Use the adverb forms of the adjectives in parentheses.
> On December 17, 1903, the Wright brothers tested their first plane. They shook hands **¹ [nervously]** *(nervous)* as Orville got into the plane.
> The plane started to move **² [slowly]** *(slow)* along the ground.
> Then the plane started to move **³ [quickly]** *(quick)* and after a moment, it **⁴ [suddenly]** *(sudden)* lifted up into the air. Orville was lying on the bottom wing of the plane. He was holding onto the plane **⁵ [tightly]** *(tight)* and he was watching the people on the ground. The people were cheering **⁶ [loudly]** *(loud)* and waving at Orville **⁷ [excitedly]** *(excited)*.

### 【p.73 練習與篇章填空 Practice】

#### C. Choose the correct words to complete the conversation.
*(插圖說明：右上角展示兩名中東/地中海面孔的學童大頭照，女孩 Nada 圍著藍色圍巾，男孩 Ali 穿著紅色圓領衫微笑。)*

- **Nada**: What's your favorite invention, Ali?
- **Ali**: I love computers. They're brilliant. Computers help us do everything **¹ [c. more easily]**.
- **Nada**: What was the world like before computers?
- **Ali**: Well, I think children did their homework **² [a. more slowly]** than we do, because they didn't have the Internet!
- **Nada**: That's true. Today, we can find information **³ [c. more quickly]** than people in the past. Do you use the Internet regularly?
- **Ali**: I use it **⁴ [a. more regularly]** than my brother. He never goes online. But my sister uses the Internet **⁵ [b. the most regularly]**. She's always online!
- **Nada**: Is she? Can you type fast on your computer?
- **Ali**: Yes, I can. I can type **⁶ [a. the fastest]** of all the children in my class!

**題目選項與正解：**
1. a most easier / b more than easily / **c more easily** *(正解：c)*
2. **a more slowly** / b more slower / c most slowly *(正解：a)*
3. a most quickly / b more quicker / **c more quickly** *(正解：c)*
4. **a more regularly** / b most regularly / c more regular *(正解：a)*
5. a the more regularly / **b the most regularly** / c most regularly than *(正解：b)*
6. **a the fastest** / b faster than / c the faster *(正解：a)*

    adverb_formation_rules:
      regular: "形容詞 + ly (quick -> quickly, quiet -> quietly)"
      ends_in_y: "去 y 變 i + ly (easy -> easily, heavy -> heavily, happy -> happily)"
      ends_in_le: "去 e 加 y (terrible -> terribly, horrible -> horribly)"
      irregular_identical: "同形副詞 (fast, hard, early, late, high, far)"
      irregular_special: "good -> well, bad -> badly"
    comparison_formulas:
      comparative: "more + 多音節副詞 + than (more quickly than) / 不規則 (better, worse, further than)"
      superlative: "the most + 多音節副詞 (the most quickly) / the best, the furthest, the fastest"
      
  misconceptions_and_error_traps:
    - trap_id: "ERR_ADVERB_ADJECTIVE_CONFUSION"
      error_pattern: "*He ran quick. / *She sings beautiful."
      correction: "He ran quickly. / She sings beautifully."
      explanation: "修飾動詞動作必須用副詞 (-ly)，修飾名詞才用形容詞。"
      
    - trap_id: "ERR_FAST_HARD_WITH_LY"
      error_pattern: "*He drives fastly. / *She works hardly."
      correction: "He drives fast. / She works hard."
      explanation: "fast 沒有 fastly！hardly 是『幾乎不』而非『努力地』，是段考極經典陷阱。"
      
    - trap_id: "ERR_DOUBLE_COMPARATIVE_ADVERB"
      error_pattern: "*more faster than / *more quicker than"
      correction: "faster than / more quickly than"
      explanation: "不能在 -er 比較級前重複加 more。"
      
    - trap_id: "ERR_GOOD_WELL_CONFUSION"
      error_pattern: "*He plays tennis good. / *He is a well player."
      correction: "He plays tennis well. / He is a good player."
      explanation: "good 是形容詞，well 是副詞修飾動詞 plays。"

  assessment_ladder:
    level_1_recognition:

      answer: "late (遲到地；lately 是『最近』)"

      prompt: "Ken ran _______ (fast) than Peter, but Leo ran _______ (fast) of all."
      answer: "faster; the fastest"
    level_3_error_correction:
      prompt: "找出錯誤並改正：'My sister speaks English more better than my brother.'"
      correction: "My sister speaks English better than my brother. (刪除 more，better 本身已是比較級)"
