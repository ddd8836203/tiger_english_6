### 1. Topic 3 Intro: The Mighty Compass (Student Book p.35)

The first compasses were used about eight hundred years ago. A compass is an instrument you use to tell direction. It has a small, pointed magnet that always points north. If you know where north is, you can always find south, west, and east. Use a compass with a map, and you can find your way anywhere!

- **Questions**:
  1. What is a compass?
  2. How can you use a compass if you get lost?
  3. Have you ever used a map? Where?

### 2. Unit 5 Reading Goal & Get Ready (Student Book p.36)

> [!IMPORTANT] **READING GOAL: Identify and Analyze the Setting**
> The setting is where and when a story happens. It can affect a story's characters and events. While you read, think about the setting. Ask yourself, *How does the writer describe it? How does it affect the characters and plot?*

### 3. Story: Lost and Found (Student Book p.36 - p.37)

```text
Lost and Found

[Line 1]  Jaya and her family went hiking one foggy morning.
[Line 2]  Their goal was a hill with a tall, crooked tree on top.
[Line 3]  They liked to picnic there.
[Line 4]  
[Line 5]  "I think we missed our turn," said Jaya. She pointed to a
[Line 6]  log bench on the left side of the trail.
[Line 7]  "Yeah, I don't remember this bench. There's a place we
[Line 8]  should have turned right, but I think we went straight,"
[Line 9]  said her brother, Manoj.
[Line 10] Their mom looked at her watch, and added, "And
[Line 11] it's noon. We should be there by now."
[Line 12] They stopped and looked around at the trees. Jaya
[Line 13] realized they were next to a different, smaller hill. She
[Line 14] said, "Before we go back down the trail, we should hike
[Line 15] up the hill and take a look around!"
[Line 16] "I'm tired," said Manoj. "I don't want to climb an extra hill."
[Line 17] "Why not? Let's go!" said their mom. So they walked up, and
[Line 18] they discovered the top of the hill was above the fog. "Nice!"
[Line 19] Mom said. "We know how to read a map, so let's look. There's the
[Line 20] lighthouse near town. We know that's south, and I think we're on
[Line 21] a hill between the lighthouse and our usual picnic spot."
[Line 22] They looked at the map. Then they looked around and saw the
[Line 23] top of another hill to the northeast. It had a tall, crooked tree
[Line 24] on top.
[Line 25] "There it is!" said Jaya. "It looks like an island in a sea of clouds."
```

> **[Setting Analysis Note p.37]**:
> - **Time**: `one foggy morning` ➔ `noon` (正午 12 點)
> - **Place/Landscape**: `hiking trail`, `log bench`, `smaller hill above fog`, `lighthouse to the south`, `destination hill with crooked tree to the northeast`
> - **Metaphor**: `"It looks like an island in a sea of clouds."` (霧海中的島嶼)

### 4. Understand (Student Book p.38 - p.39)

#### A. Think about the setting. Answer the questions and discuss. (p.38)
1. **Where is the family in the story? What is the weather like?**
   - *Answer*: `They are on a hiking trail in the hills/mountains. The weather is very foggy.`
2. **How do Jaya and Manoj know they're in the wrong place?**
   - *Answer*: `They see a log bench on the left side of the trail that they don't remember, realizing they should have turned right instead of going straight.`
3. **What time of day is it when Jaya and her family see the bench?**
   - *Answer*: `It is noon (12:00 PM).`
4. **How does weather affect the family's plans?**
   - *Answer*: `The thick fog makes it impossible to see distant landmarks, causing them to miss their trail turn and lose their way.`

#### B. Choose the correct answer. (p.38)
1. What does the family do on the hill?
   - [ ] a. sit down
   - [ ] b. turn left
   - [x] **c. read a map**
   - [ ] d. have a picnic
2. Where is the fog?
   - [x] **a. at the bottom of the hill** *(the top of the hill was above the fog)*
   - [ ] b. above the hill
   - [ ] c. to the north
   - [ ] d. off the main trail
3. How did Jaya and her family get on the wrong trail?
   - [ ] a. They went left.
   - [x] **b. They went straight.** *(instead of turning right)*
   - [ ] c. They went up a hill.
   - [ ] d. They went back.
4. Where did Jaya and her family plan to hike to?
   - [ ] a. the bench
   - [ ] b. the lighthouse
   - [ ] c. the hill above the fallen log
   - [x] **d. the hill with the crooked tree**

#### D. Complete the sentences. (p.39)
*Word Bank*: `between` | `go back` | `go straight` | `next to` | `on the left` | `read a map` | `turn right` | `up the hill`

My mom, my sister, and I went hiking in our favorite park last weekend. We usually (1) **turn right** at a place where the main trail splits. On this trip, we decided to (2) **go straight** instead of turning.  
We knew where we were going, so we didn't need to (3) **read a map**. Our plan was to (4) **go back** to an old, giant tree we once found by accident.  
We walked until we saw a bench (5) **on the left** side of the trail, near a tall hill. We were going to hike (6) **up the hill**, but it was a long climb, so we rested.  
I sat down (7) **next to** my sister on the bench. Mom sat down (8) **between** us. But after only one minute, she asked, "Are you ready to hike?"

#### E. Informational Nonfiction Reading: Warning! Warning! (p.39)
```text
Warning! Warning!
A lighthouse is a tall tower built on the coast. The top of a lighthouse has a huge lamp and a very loud horn. The light from the lamp can be seen far out on the ocean. The horn can be heard far away. When fog forms, someone goes to the lighthouse and turns on the light and horn. This warns people in ships where the land is, telling them which direction not to go.
```
- **Comprehension Questions & Key**:
  1. A lighthouse is a tall **tower** built on the coast.
  2. It has a bright **lamp** and very loud **horn**.
  3. These are turned on when **fog** forms along the coast.
  4. A lighthouse warns **people in ships** that they are near land.
