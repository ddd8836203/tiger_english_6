### 1. Reading Goal & Get Ready (Student Book p.54)

> [!IMPORTANT] **READING GOAL: Make Predictions**
> A prediction is what you think will happen. While you read, use clues in the story and your own knowledge to guess what might happen. Make predictions throughout the story and at the end of the story.

#### Get Ready
**A. Read the sentences below. Choose the correct answer.**
1. Which sentence is a prediction?
   - [x] **a. Darla will do well in school.** *(含有 will 未來預測動詞)*
   - [ ] b. Darla fell asleep on the rug. *(過去已發生之事實)*
2. Ron sent a text message. What will happen next?
   - [ ] a. He will send the message.
   - [x] **b. His friend will read the message.** *(基於生活常理之合理預測)*

`Italian`, `British accent`, `Portuguese`, `Australia`, `Brazil`, `bilingual`, `Australian accent`.

### 3. Understand (Student Book p.56 - p.57)

#### A. Think about making predictions. Answer the questions. (p.56)
1. **Read the paragraph starting at line 4. What class do you think Rosa is going to?**
   - *Answer*: `She is going to an art class, because she is carrying a big pile of drawing paper, paint brushes, and colored pencils.`
2. **How did you think Erico would answer Rosa's questions? Did he answer the way you thought he would?**
   - *Answer*: `Erico was very polite and patient. He realized that asking questions was Rosa's way of being friendly, rather than being rude.`
3. **What do you predict will happen after the story ends?**
   - *Answer*: `Erico and Rosa will walk into the art classroom together, and Erico will tell her the long story of why he grew up with a British accent.`

#### B. Read. Choose T for True and F for False. (p.56)
1. Rosa first speaks to Erico in Portuguese. ➔ **F** *(Rosa 開口第一句話是用義大利語 Italian 發問)*
2. Rosa and Erico are studying art. ➔ **T** *(從畫紙、筆刷與色鉛筆等美術用品推斷)*
3. Erico was born in Australia. ➔ **T** *(他的家庭來自澳洲 Australia)*
4. Erico's parents were born in different countries. ➔ **T** *(父親來自巴西 Brazil，母親來自葡萄牙 Portugal)*
5. Erico decides Rosa is unfriendly. ➔ **F** *(Erico 明白連珠發問是 Rosa 展現友善的方式)*
6. Knowing Portuguese helped Erico learn Italian. ➔ **T** *(第 35 行明確肯定：Portuguese did help me learn some Italian)*
7. Erico speaks with an Australian accent. ➔ **F** *(他帶著英國腔 British accent)*
8. Erico has a long story to tell Rosa. ➔ **T** *(文章最後一句：do you want to hear a long story?)*

#### D. Context Clues & Word Meanings: Choose the correct answer. (p.57)
1. In line 2, **Italian** means a language from:
   - [ ] a. Australia.
   - [ ] b. Brazil.

   - [ ] d. Portugal.
2. In line 10, **accent** means a way to:
   - [ ] a. draw or paint.
   - [x] **b. pronounce words.**

3. In line 18, **Portuguese** means from:

4. In line 22, **bilingual** means:
