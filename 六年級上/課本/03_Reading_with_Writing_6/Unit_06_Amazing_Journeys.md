[Line 14] Controls are placed

### 3. Understand (Student Book p.42 - p.43)

#### A. Think about the visuals. Answer the questions. (p.42)
1. **How many visuals are there? What do they show?**
   - *Answer*: `There are two visuals. Figure 1 shows an orienteering control flag hanging on a tree. Figure 2 shows an orienteering control card with 18 control boxes.`
2. **What does a control flag look like?**
   - *Answer*: `It is a bright flag with red/orange and white triangles.`
3. **Look at the card. How many controls are there?**
   - *Answer*: `There are 18 controls.`
4. **How do visuals help you to understand a text?**
   - *Answer*: `Visuals provide visual details (what the equipment looks like, how many stations there are) that are not fully described in the text.`

#### B. Read. Choose T for True and F for False. (p.42)
1. Orienteering events are held outdoors. ➔ **T** *(舉辦於森林原野等戶外)*
2. In orienteering, you may only use a compass. ➔ **F** *(必須同時使用地圖與指南針 a map and a compass)*
3. Control flags have bright colors. ➔ **T** *(紅白或橘白亮色)*
4. Control flags are easy to find. ➔ **F** *(刻意藏在巨石後或大樹旁，從各角度不容易直接看見)*
5. Racers carry cards that they mark. ➔ **T** *(攜帶控制卡打卡)*
6. Racers must know how to read a map. ➔ **T** *(必須具備地圖判讀能力)*
7. Racers need a map and compass to find the finish line. ➔ **T** *(終點線同樣需要指北針定位)*
8. You must finish first to win an orienteering event. ➔ **F** *(必須「打滿全卡所有控制點」的第一名才算獲勝)*
