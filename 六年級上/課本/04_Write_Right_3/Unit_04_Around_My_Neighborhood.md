#### Writing Task: Write about your neighborhood.

### 【p.31 範文精讀 Model Text】

#### Cynthia's Story — My Neighborhood
*(範文配圖：便籤紙排版手寫體風格，右下角附有一張真實城鎮俯瞰照片，展現整齊的磚紅色公寓與綠意街道。)*

> **Have you ever been to my neighborhood?**
> **I have lived in my neighborhood for three years.** It is very close to my school. I can get to school in ten minutes on foot. The subway station and the bus stop are very close, too.
> There are many stores in my neighborhood. The grocery store is two blocks from my house. Across the street from the grocery store, there is a hair salon, a dry cleaner's and a bank. Best of all, my neighborhood has a park. My friends and I often have lunch there.
> **I really like living in my neighborhood!**

#### Answer the Questions (閱讀理解問答)
1. **How long has Cynthia lived in her neighborhood?**
   ➔ **She has lived in her neighborhood for three years.**
2. **What do Cynthia and her friends do at the park?**
   ➔ **They often have lunch there.**

### 【p.33 方位介系詞 Writing Basics II】

#### Look at the map. Write the sentences using the phrases in the box.
*(地圖空間方位線索題，練習 6 組高頻方位片語)*

> **方位片語庫：**
> - `across the street from` *(在...對街)*
> - `around the corner from` *(在...街角拐角處)*
> - `next to` *(在...隔壁相鄰)*
> - `down the street from` *(沿著這條街下去)*
> - `two blocks from` *(距離...兩個街區)*
> - `in front of` *(在...正前方)*

- **[範例] (the grocery store / my house)**
  ➔ **The grocery store is two blocks from my house.**
1. **(the stationery store / the gas station)**
   ➔ **The stationery store is next to the gas station.**
2. **(the hair salon / the dry cleaner's)**
   ➔ **The hair salon is across the street from the dry cleaner's.**
3. **(the Internet café / the grocery store)**
   ➔ **The Internet café is down the street from the grocery store.**
4. **(the coffee shop / the hair salon)**
   ➔ **The coffee shop is around the corner from the hair salon.**
5. **(the bus stop / the park)**
   ➔ **The bus stop is in front of the park.**
6. **(the pharmacy / the convenience store)**
   ➔ **The pharmacy is down the street from the convenience store.** *(或 around the corner from...)*

### 【p.34 寫作實戰練習 Writing Practice I】

#### A. Look at the example. Change the sentences in the same way.
*(現在完成式轉換：過去時間點 moved ago ➔ 延續狀態 have lived for)*

- **[Example] I moved here three years ago.**
  ➔ **I have lived here for three years.**
1. I moved here two years ago.
   ➔ **I have lived here for two years.**
2. My grandparents moved here 20 years ago.
   ➔ **My grandparents have lived here for 20 years.**
3. Mr. and Mrs. Beck moved here six years ago.
   ➔ **Mr. and Mrs. Beck have lived here for six years.**
4. Daniel moved here 10 years ago.
   ➔ **Daniel has lived here for 10 years.** *(注意單數 has)*
5. My uncle moved here two months ago.
   ➔ **My uncle has lived here for two months.**

#### B. Look at the example. Write the sentences in the same way.
*(交通工具與花費時間句型：I can get to... in [時間] by [交通工具] / on foot)*

- **[Example] (library / 25 minutes / by bus)**
  ➔ **I can get to the library in 25 minutes by bus.**
1. **(the park / 15 minutes / by bicycle)**
   ➔ **I can get to the park in 15 minutes by bicycle.**
2. **(the shopping mall / 30 minutes / by subway)**
   ➔ **I can get to the shopping mall in 30 minutes by subway.**
3. **(the movie theater / an hour / by car)**
   ➔ **I can get to the movie theater in an hour by car.**
4. **(my school / 10 minutes / on foot)**
   ➔ **I can get to my school in 10 minutes on foot.** *(注意步行必用 on foot)*
5. **(my best friend's house / 20 minutes / on foot)**
   ➔ **I can get to my best friend's house in 20 minutes on foot.**

### 【p.35 寫作實戰練習 Writing Practice II】

#### A. Match the phrases to make correct sentences.
1. My friends and I have lunch ➔ **at the park.**
2. I keep my money ➔ **at the bank.**
3. I get my hair cut ➔ **at the hair salon.**
4. I buy my school supplies ➔ **at the stationery store.**
5. My mother buys food ➔ **at the grocery store.**
6. My father has his suits cleaned ➔ **at the dry cleaner's.**

#### B. Write the sentences using the answers above.
1. My neighborhood has a park. ➔ **My friends and I have lunch there.** *(範例)*
2. My neighborhood has a grocery store. ➔ **My mother buys food there.**
3. My neighborhood has a bank. ➔ **I keep my money there.**
4. My neighborhood has a hair salon. ➔ **I get my hair cut there.**
5. My neighborhood has a dry cleaner's. ➔ **My father has his suits cleaned there.**
6. My neighborhood has a stationery store. ➔ **I buy my school supplies there.**

#### C. Unscramble the sentences. (重組句子)
1. ever been / my / to / Have / neighborhood / you / ?
   ➔ **Have you ever been to my neighborhood?**
2. are / the bus stop / close, / and / The subway station / too / very / .
   ➔ **The subway station and the bus stop are very close, too.**
3. are / neighborhood / in / stores / There / my / many / .
   ➔ **There are many stores in my neighborhood.**
4. really / living / I / my neighborhood / like / in / .
   ➔ **I really like living in my neighborhood!**
