### 2. Model Text: Nathan's Story (Student Book p.39)

```text
If I Had 300 Dollars

If I had 300 dollars, I would do three things.
With the first 100 dollars, I would buy a nice present for my mom. My mom loves scarves, so I would get her a beautiful scarf.
Next, I would donate 100 dollars to a charity group. I think you should help other people whenever you can.
Finally, I would spend 100 dollars on myself. A baseball glove and some comic books would cost me about 70 dollars. With the rest of the money, I would treat my friends to lunch.
Wouldn't it be great to have 300 dollars?
```

- **Answer the Questions**:
  1. What would Nathan do with the first 100 dollars?
     - *Handwritten Answer*: `He would buy a nice present for his mom. / He would get her a beautiful scarf.`
  2. How much would a baseball glove and some comic books cost Nathan?
     - *Handwritten Answer*: `They would cost him about 70 dollars.`

### 6. Writing Practice II (Student Book p.43)

#### Part A: Look at the example. Write the sentences in the same way.
- **[Example]**: (the rest / go to the movies)
  ➔ `With the rest of the money, I would go to the movies.`
- **Sentences & Key**:
  1. (some / treat my friends to lunch)
     ➔ `With some of the money, I would treat my friends to lunch.`
  2. (most / open up a bank account)
     ➔ `With most of the money, I would open up a bank account.`
  3. (all / buy school supplies)
     ➔ `With all of the money, I would buy school supplies.`
  4. (half / buy dinner for my family)
     ➔ `With half of the money, I would buy dinner for my family.`
  5. (a third / get a new book bag)
     ➔ `With a third of the money, I would get a new book bag.`

#### Part B: Correct the mistakes and rewrite the sentences.
1. `If I had 300 dollars, I will do three things.`
   - **Correction**: `If I had 300 dollars, I would do three things.` *(will 改為 would，虛擬語氣主要子句一致性)*
2. `I would buy a nice present to my mom.`
   - **Correction**: `I would buy a nice present for my mom.` *(buy 某物「給」某人用介系詞 for)*
3. `I would donate 100 dollar to a charity group.`
   - **Correction**: `I would donate 100 dollars to a charity group.` *(超過 1 元的複數單位名詞須加 s: dollars)*
4. `Wouldn't it great to have 300 dollars?`
   - **Correction**: `Wouldn't it be great to have 300 dollars?` *(情態助動詞 would 後接形容詞 great 時，必須補上原形 be 動詞)*

### 8. Drafting & Writing Tip! (Student Book p.45)

#### Drafting Template:
```text
Title: [ If I Had 300 Dollars ]

Introduction:
If I had [300 dollars], I would [do three things].

Body:
(Paragraph 1 - First)
With [the first 100 dollars], I would [buy a nice present for my mom].
[My mom loves scarves], so I [would get her a beautiful scarf].

(Paragraph 2 - Next)
Next, I would [donate 100 dollars to a charity group].
I think [you should help other people whenever you can].

(Paragraph 3 - Finally)
Finally, I would [spend 100 dollars on myself].
[A baseball glove and some comic books] would cost me [about 70 dollars].
With the rest of the money, I would [treat my friends to lunch].

Conclusion:
Wouldn't it be [great to have 300 dollars]?
```

#### Writing Tip! Writing about unreal facts:
> [!IMPORTANT] **Writing Tip! (Unreal Facts 虛擬假設語氣)**
> When you write about something that is unreal, you use the 'if' clause in the past tense. In the main clause that follows the 'if' clause in the past tense, you use **'would'** instead of **'will'**.
> - *If I **had** 300 dollars, I **would**...*
> - *If I **lived** in Sweden, I **would**...*
> 
> Remember that you usually use **'were'** instead of **'was'** in the 'if' clause:
> - *If I **were** a millionaire, I **would**...*
> - *If she **were** here, she **would**...*
